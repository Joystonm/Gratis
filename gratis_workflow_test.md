# Gratis Primary Workflow Test

## Objective

Verify the main Gratis design workflow:

Create → Edit → Export → Upload → Apply image tools.

## URL

http://localhost:5173

## Image

u can upload image from this path : F:\Gratis\Images\headphones.jpg

## Steps

### 1. Open Gratis

Open http://localhost:5173.

Verify Gratis branding and the Create Design button.

### 2. Create design

Click Create Design.

Create a custom 1080 × 1080 design.

Verify the editor, canvas, toolbar, and properties panel are visible.

### 3. Add text

Open Text and add a Heading.

Change the text to "Gratis Design Test".

Verify it appears on the canvas.

### 4. Add shape

Open Shapes and add a Rectangle.

Change its fill colour.

Verify the rectangle and colour change.

### 5. Export

Click Export.

Verify PNG, JPG, or WebP options are available.

Export the design.

### 6. Upload image

Open Uploads.

Upload:

F:\Gratis\Images\headphones.jpg

Verify the headphones image appears on the canvas.

### 7. Apply Local Tools

Select the headphones image.

Open Local Tools.

Apply Grayscale and verify the image becomes black and white.

Apply Sepia Tone and verify the sepia effect.

### 8. Verify Cloudinary Image Tools

Open Image Tools.

Verify the Cloudinary panel is visible.

Verify Filters, Adjust, and Optimize tabs are available.

Open Filters and verify filter options are visible.

Open Adjust and verify Brightness, Contrast, Saturation, and Tint controls.

Open Optimize and verify quality and format options.
